## 📌 About

**Sentinel** is an AI-assisted Hybrid Threat Intelligence dashboard designed to help analysts **identify, prioritize, and explore potentially suspicious or coordinated activity** through multiple intelligence signals.

The dashboard combines risk scoring, anomaly-related indicators, sentiment signals, narrative analysis, and interactive visualizations into a single analytical interface.

> **Sentinel is a research and portfolio proof-of-concept using synthetic intelligence scenarios.**

## 📊 Dashboard

The Sentinel dashboard provides an interactive overview of threat-related intelligence through multiple analytical views.

### Key Views

- **Threat Level** — overview of detected risk levels
- **Risk Score** — prioritization of intelligence records
- **Sentiment Analysis** — sentiment-related intelligence signals
- **Activity Timeline** — temporal activity patterns
- **Narrative Analysis** — exploration of recurring narratives
- **Relationship Graph** — connections between platforms, narratives, accounts and indicators
- **Interactive Filtering** — dynamic exploration of the intelligence dataset
- **Analyst Summary** — concise overview of the current intelligence picture

---

## 🧠 Risk Scoring

Sentinel uses a transparent, rule-based scoring approach to combine multiple intelligence signals.

The current model considers:

- Bot score
- Engagement anomaly
- Sentiment signals
- Suspicious keywords
- URL indicators
- Coordination signals

Conceptually:

```text
Risk Score
    │
    ├── Bot Signal
    ├── Engagement Anomaly
    ├── Sentiment
    ├── Suspicious Keywords
    ├── URL Indicator
    └── Coordination Signal
            │
            ▼
      Risk Prioritization
```

The score is intended for **analytical triage and prioritization**, not automated attribution.

## 🕸️ Threat Intelligence Analysis

Sentinel brings multiple signals together to help analysts explore relationships such as:

```text
Platform
    ↓
Narrative
    ↓
Accounts
    ↓
Indicators
    ↓
Potentially Coordinated Activity
```

The goal is to provide a **single visual analytical environment** rather than isolated indicators.

---

## 🔐 Responsible Use

Sentinel is a **research and educational proof-of-concept**.

- Uses synthetic intelligence scenarios
- Does not access private accounts
- Does not access classified intelligence
- Does not perform surveillance
- Does not perform real-world attribution
- Does not make autonomous operational decisions

Risk scores represent **analytical signals**, not definitive evidence of malicious activity.
